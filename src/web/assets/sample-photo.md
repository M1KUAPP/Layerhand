## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup

Asset type: bundled sample photograph for a photo-retouching web application.

Primary request: an original, photorealistic unretouched studio product
photograph that visibly benefits from professional retouching.

Scene and backdrop: warm off-white paper sweep with a few realistic wrinkles
and a faint seam.

Subject: one unbranded cobalt-blue glass cosmetic bottle with a plain
brushed-metal cap.

Style and medium: realistic commercial product photography, deliberately raw
rather than polished.

Composition and framing: horizontal 3:2 composition, product centered with
comfortable margins, entire bottle visible.

Lighting and mood: soft side-lit studio daylight with believable glass
reflections and a restrained cast shadow.

Materials and textures: authentic glass, brushed metal, paper grain, a few tiny
dust specks and mild reflection distractions suitable for a retouching demo.

Constraints: no text, no label, no logo, no trademark, no watermark, no
additional objects, no dramatic gradient, and no already-perfect retouching.
